# Shaders
What are shaders
----------------

Shaders are programs to color a canvas on screen. Taking inputs like the resolution, geometry etc. and outputting a color for every pixel on screen. 

How you should be thinking about shaders
----------------------------------------

It's an incredibly deep technical subject. The basics are simple but the final result is incredibly layered. You should be thinking of them as different recipes like uv, voronoi, noise or whatev. Different blocks of concepts that you string together
