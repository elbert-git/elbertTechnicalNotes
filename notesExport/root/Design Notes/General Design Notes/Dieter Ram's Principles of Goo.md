# Dieter Ram's Principles of Good Design
Is Innovative
-------------

And not just for the sake of it. Just like Twitter's pull to refresh was new at it's time. it was natural and almost a no-brainer. That said dont make buttons triangular instead of rectangles for that sake of innovation

Is Useful
---------

Usability and ergonomics. No sharp headache inducing contrast. Everything is neatly and intentionally spaced out for reading

is Aesthetic
------------

Correct use of colours shapes and well generally looking nice. not (unintentionally) breaking any design rules

Is Long-Lasting
---------------

Trends are temporary, fundamentals are forever. A good design done with a good undestanding of the fundementals will always be timelessly good

Is Understandable
-----------------

Just like usefulness, it should be a no-brainer and able to be understood at a glance

Is Unobtrusive
--------------

It's not about the design, rather what it enables. good design shoudl be invisible. Not like some dribble eye-candy that are more not very usable in reality

Is Honest
---------

Conveying data is what UI design is all about. Don't hide or contort data to fit visual needs

Is Thorough
-----------

Everything should be intentional. No unintended choice. Like the small X to close the window. 

Is Environmentally Friendly
---------------------------

Efficient. not wasteful at all

Is little design
----------------

Design is a means to and end. not the end itself. the simplest and most elegant solution is the best
