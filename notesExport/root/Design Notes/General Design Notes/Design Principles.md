# Design Principles
Like the 12 principles of animations.  These can be applied as checklists to your proces

| Principle | Explanation |
| --- | --- |
| Contrast | Allow Elements to be emphasized |
| proportion & hierachy | Hierarchy creates a flow. From most emphasized to least |
| Variation & repetition | Do you want things to feel balanced or in tension |
| White space | For things to not feel cramped |
