Mortgage backed securities  
\- in early 2000s, investors looking for new safe sources of returns.   
\- stock and bonds are still good but in the dot com crash of 2000 shows that you cant reliably get money from bankrupt firms  
\- People start looking at mortgages because they act like bonds but they are backed with actual property. If loans are defaulted then the house still saves the value  
\- investment banks start selling mortgage backed securities  
\- this kicks mortgage lending and home buying into hyperdrive  
\- Enter Credit default swaps  
\- derivatives on mortgages defaulting. a bet  
\- and well this balloons money for companies but it exposes them to extreme risk if things go south  
\- this means if real estate crashes. everything crashes  
\- here's the kicker. because lenders are selling their loans. they are incentivised to have more risky loans at all costs.   
\- These are sub prime loans.   
\- loan given out almost willy nilly allowing the any tom dick and harry to get a house loan.   
\- this entices people to get a house they cant afford. almost making them guaranteed to default  
\- housing prices fall  
\- this evaporates any value of mortgages.
